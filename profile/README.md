## Why we are on GitHub

Most of what we make is for Central: the practical tools that help us gather, communicate, and serve. Some of that work is useful beyond our own church family.

This organisation is where we publish it.

You are welcome to use what you find here, fork it, and adapt it for your own context. One of our values is to be generous with all God gives — including the work of our hands.

## What you will find here

This space is new. As we release tools, they will live in this organisation as public repositories, each with its own README so you can run it, change it, and contribute back.

We expect to share things like:

- **Church operations** — small apps and scripts that take friction out of Sunday-to-Sunday life
- **Communications** — helpers for how we write, design, and share with our church family
- **Open resources** — templates, workflows, and pieces of infrastructure other churches can reuse

If a repository is here, it is meant to be used.

## Using and contributing

You do not need to be part of Central to use what we publish.

- **Use it** — clone or fork a repository and follow its README
- **Adapt it** — churches are not identical; change it for your context
- **Contribute** — issues and pull requests are welcome. If you have fixed something or made it better, we would love to hear from you

If you are on the team at Central and want to publish something, talk to us first so we can keep this organisation clear and well looked after.

## About Central

We are a community of ordinary people in Bristol, trying to follow Jesus and to love the world as He does. Our mission is simple: **helping the people of Bristol to follow Jesus**.

We gather at **10.30am** (in-person and online) and **5.30pm** (in-person) on Sundays. You would be so welcome.

> Encounter God. Redeem life. Love our city.
